Site Plan

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide a professional, informative platform for LogiTrack Freight Solutions to showcase their logistics services, enable easy shipment tracking, and provide contact and location information for clients.

User Scenarios

  • A business owner seeking reliable freight shipping options.
  • A customer tracking a shipment status quickly.
  • A visitor finding contact details and office location.
  • A client requesting a custom quote for services.

Color Scheme

The site uses a warm orange (#e76f51) as the primary color, paired with neutral backgrounds (#f5f5f5, white) and dark text (#333) for readability and professionalism.

Typography

Headings use a bold, modern sans-serif font for clarity and emphasis. Body text uses a clean, easy-to-read sans-serif font for accessibility and user comfort.

Wireframes

The wireframe sketches focus on a simple, user-friendly layout with a fixed navigation bar, hero section, service highlights, and footer with contact info.
